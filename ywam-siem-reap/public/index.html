Discipleship Training School

SIX MONTHS
THAT ASK
EVERYTHING.

Twelve weeks of lecture phase in Siem Reap, then twelve weeks of outreach. Same group, start to finish. You'll be up early, you'll cook and clean on a rota, and you'll have your assumptions handled roughly and kindly.

DTS is the door into everything else here — staffing, media, the schools, long-term work in Cambodia.

PHASE 01
Lecture phase · 12 weeks · Siem Reap

Mornings in class with a different speaker each week. Afternoons in the community. Evenings: your housemates, your questions, and a lot of rice.

PHASE 02
Outreach phase · 12 weeks · on the road

You leave Siem Reap. One month in other Cambodian provinces, one month in another country, and the weeks around them preparing and debriefing as a team.

Practicalព័ត៌មានជាក់ស្តែង

The questions everybody asks
How old do I need to be?

Eighteen and up. Most students are 18–25; every intake has a few people in their thirties and they are usually the glue.

Where does outreach go?

One month in other Cambodian provinces, one month in another country. The destination is set during the lecture phase and depends on where the school is invited.

Do I need to speak Khmer?

No. Lectures are in English with translation as needed. You'll pick up enough Khmer to be teased about it.

Do I have to be a Christian?

DTS is built for people who want to follow Jesus and don't know what that looks like yet. Questions are welcome; pretending isn't necessary.

How do I pay for it?

Most students raise support from family, friends and their church. We'll send a plan that works — people do this every intake, including students with no church behind them.

I'm Cambodian — is this for me?

Yes, and local rates apply. Message us in Khmer and a Khmer staff member will answer — including your parents' questions.
